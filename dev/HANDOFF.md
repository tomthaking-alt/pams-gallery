# Pam's Gallery: where things stand (9 November 2026)

## What the site does today (all live at itsamepameo.com)

**For visitors**
- A walk-through 3D gallery: sunlit Greek halls, an opening film, King George the guide bird and a balcony.
  Each collection has its own wall, and every painting hangs in a frame chosen to suit it (15 frame styles).
- Painting windows with zoom, sharing, and "View on your wall":
  - choose your live camera or a photo of any wall
  - the painting appears as a 3D framed object, with 13 frames to try and a turn control
  - on iPhone and Android, the first six paintings also offer a true-size AR viewer
- A Collections page with a "Just arrived" section, plus Prints, Commissions, Galleries & press (with a press kit),
  Buying with confidence and Privacy pages.
- A page of its own for every painting (`/art/<name>`), an All works page (`/works`), a sitemap, link previews and a proper 404 page.
  These work without JavaScript.
- Enquiries, offers, auctions and bids, messages and commission requests, all with spam protection. After each one,
  people see clear "what happens next" steps.
- The collectors' circle (free membership): early access to new paintings for a window Pam sets (currently
  48 hours), and a signed print for completing a collection. Visitors see a blurred preview and a countdown.
- An email sign-up with proper consent (it asks people to confirm by email once email is switched on), and an unsubscribe link in every email.
- Watermarks on anything downloadable; right-click and long-press saving blocked; copyright details inside every picture file.
- Home-screen icon, an offline page, smaller WebP pictures, a faster opening with a percentage, a simple view for
  slow or older devices, music only when chosen, and keyboard and screen-reader support.

**For Pam (Dashboard)**
- Orders and enquiries, messages, members, the mailing list, paintings (prices, sizes, frames, members-only dates),
  collections, site text, settings (including Buying & delivery and the early-access window) and a Grow plan.
- **Visitors**: live "active now", unique visitors, page views, average visit, bounce rate, a trend chart, sources
  with conversion rates, countries, devices and browsers, and most-viewed paintings, for any date range in any timezone.
  Bots and Pam's own visits are left out. Counting started on 9 November 2026.

**Quality checks** (last run 9 November 2026): about 120 automatic checks pass. Mobile PageSpeed for /works scored
95 for performance, 100 for accessibility, 100 for best practices and 100 for SEO.
